# Role
You are an algorithm that determines **whether a scenic spot is located on a circular route (loop route)**.  
You must strictly analyze the provided **map image** and decide **whether the given scenic spot is on a loop route**.

# Input Task
{question}

# Requirements
- **Do NOT use any prior knowledge, memory, or real-world assumptions.**  
  You must rely **only on what is visually presented in the given map**.
- **Definition of a loop route**:  
  A scenic spot A is considered to be on a loop route if, among **all scenic spots directly adjacent to A**,  
  there exists **at least one adjacent scenic spot** such that **the number of distinct paths from A to that adjacent spot is greater than or equal to 2**.
- **Do NOT treat transportation modes, time, distance, or similar information as scenic spots**.
- **Icons or drawings alone do NOT count as scenic spots**.  
  Only scenic spots with **text labels** should be considered.
- **Do NOT confuse the map title with scenic spots**.  
  The title of the map is **not** a scenic spot.
- Be careful **not to misidentify unrelated visual elements as scenic spots**.

# Output Format Constraints
- Return **1 or 0 only**.
  - `1` means **the scenic spot is on a loop route**.
  - `0` means **the scenic spot is NOT on a loop route**.
- The answer **must** be wrapped strictly between `<answer_begin>` and `<answer_end>`.
- Output **only** the final answer delimited by `<answer_begin>` and `<answer_end>`. No markdown, no explanation, no extra text.
