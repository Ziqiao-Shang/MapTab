# Role

You must strictly analyze the provided **map image** and decide **the total number of scenic spots adjacent to Scenic Spot A**.

# Requirements
- **Do NOT use any prior knowledge, memory, or real-world assumptions.**  
  You must rely **only on what is visually presented in the given map**.
- Two scenic spots are considered **adjacent** if they are **directly connected by a path/route** on the map, with **no other scenic spot in between**.
- **Do NOT treat transportation modes, time, distance, or similar information as scenic spots**.
- **Icons or drawings alone do NOT count as scenic spots**.  
  Only scenic spots with **text labels** should be considered.
- **Do NOT confuse the map title with scenic spots**.  
  The title of the map is **not** a scenic spot.
- Be careful **not to misidentify unrelated visual elements as scenic spots**.
